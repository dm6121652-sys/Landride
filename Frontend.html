Good to know

 

Can I cancel a ride?

Yes. You can cancel while we’re finding a driver or while the driver is on the way.

How do I pay?

Choose cash or bank transfer when you request. The fare is the price you saw before booking.

Is the ride safe?

You see your driver’s name, vehicle and plate number before they arrive. Check them before you get in.

Who do I contact about a problem?

Contact the campus transport office. Add their number or email here.
